## <img src="assets/icons/git-branch.svg" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Why his art is not on `main`

This home was set up as a gift before TMade Alex had said what he wants shown. Publishing a copy of someone's logo in
a place that carries their name, before they have said yes, could look like speaking for them. So:

- **`main`**, which is what GitHub shows, links to his own pages and copies nothing of his.
- **`with-his-art`**, a separate branch, holds copies of the first three pieces above in `logo/` and `art/`, so he can see at
  once how a brand shelf with his own files would look. It exists only on Christopher's computer. It has never been
  pushed, and it is pushed only if TMade Alex says yes. If he says no, it is never published.

Even on that branch, no folder is listed in [`PUBLIC-LANE-MEDIA.txt`](PUBLIC-LANE-MEDIA.txt): a person decides that,
never a helper.

## <img src="assets/icons/footprints.svg" width="24" height="24" alt=""> When a file arrives

A helper adds one row for it: the file's name, what it shows, where it came from, who provided it and the date. The
file keeps its bytes as they were unless the row says otherwise. A picture is shown in public only after a person
approves its folder in [`PUBLIC-LANE-MEDIA.txt`](PUBLIC-LANE-MEDIA.txt).
