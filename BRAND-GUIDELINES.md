## <img src="assets/icons/sparkles.svg" width="24" height="24" alt=""> The wordmark

- **"tradermade."**, all lower case, in white, followed by a round dot in TRADERMADE's primary cyan.
- **The letters** are a heavy, rounded geometric sans: round bowls, short ascenders, tight spacing. They read as one
  solid word, not as separate letters.
- **The dot is part of the mark.** It is the only colour in the wordmark and it sits on the baseline, like a full stop.
- **Where it appears:** centred in a thin ring on a dark circle (his YouTube channel picture and his Whop community
  picture), and large on the left of his Whop banner.
- **The name and the wordmark differ in case, on purpose.** The community is written **TRADERMADE** in text (his Whop
  store name); the wordmark is set **tradermade.** in lower case.

## <img src="assets/icons/type.svg" width="24" height="24" alt=""> The type

Three voices, as his Whop banner uses them:

| Voice | What it looks like | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| The wordmark | heavy, rounded geometric sans, lower case | "tradermade." only |
| Headlines | a clean geometric sans, medium weight, sentence case | the headline on the banner, with his name in it |
| Small lines | a monospace face in letter-spaced capitals, in mid teal or grey | the line under the wordmark, the small label above the headline, the web address |

His pages do not name the typefaces. Until he does, a draft can use any free geometric sans and monospace that look
close, and say that it is a stand-in.

## <img src="assets/icons/shapes.svg" width="24" height="24" alt=""> The dark ground

- **Dark first.** Everything sits on the deep ground, `#0c1719`, with a soft lighter glow (`#0e2224`) behind the main
  mark. There is no light version of his art today.
- **One thin line.** A hairline in mid teal (a ring around the wordmark, a rule under a headline) is the only
  decoration.
- **Room to breathe.** The wordmark has plenty of empty space around it, and nothing touches it.

## <img src="assets/icons/circle-check.svg" width="20" height="20" alt=""> How to use them

- Link to his own pages for the real thing: [YouTube @TMadeAlex](https://www.youtube.com/@TMadeAlex),
  [TRADERMADE on Whop](https://whop.com/tradermade).
- Use the colours above to make a page feel like TRADERMADE: deep ground, white headline, one touch of primary cyan.
- Keep the cyan for one thing at a time (a dot, a link, a button), as his wordmark does.
- Write the community's name as **TRADERMADE** and his as **TMade Alex**.

## <img src="assets/icons/triangle-alert.svg" width="20" height="20" alt=""> Please don't

- Copy, redraw, stretch, recolour or re-letter the wordmark, or move or recolour its dot.
- Put his wordmark or his art on anything that is not his, or on anything that suggests he endorses it.
- Use his art or photographs without his yes. On `main` here, there are none.
- Mix it up with the market-data company called TraderMade. It is unrelated, and nothing here links to it.
- Make a "TMade Alex" character, mascot or helper persona. There is one TMade Alex.
